# Automatic In-Page Side Panel

The extension now mimics the **Native Side Panel** perfectly and opens **Automatically**.

## How it works:
1. Visit `https://www.meta.ai/media`.
2. The Side Panel will slide in from the right automatically.
3. The website content will adjust to fit next to it.
4. **No clicks required.**

## Technical Note:
This uses an injected interface to simulate the browser's side panel, allowing it to bypass the "click-to-open" restriction while keeping the native look and feel.
