      Saving & sidebar

      Chrome needs a click to open its native sidebar the first time. It can then stay open across tabs.

      Local data & defaults

      Prompts, file fingerprints and media stay here until you clear them. Starting a run sends the relevant prompts and images to Meta AI / Vibes using your signed-in account.

      Defaults do not erase your prompts. Clear generated results separately in Download Manager, after saving your ZIP.

      Built-in safeguards

      • One current Meta chat and one dedicated Vibes project per run.
      • Verified start frames and numbered video candidates. No guessing or blind retries.
      • Readiness detection, not a fixed generation sleep. A timeout stops safely.
      • No Web Store updater, remote configuration, API key or telemetry.
